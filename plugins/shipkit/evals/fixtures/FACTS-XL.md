Rules for whoever edits the generator:

- The word `Redis` must appear in `PROJECT_MAP.md` and in no other generated file. Name no
  email provider anywhere, not even in a comment. The smoke check greps for both.
- The history must stay deterministic: fixed author and committer dates, no clock, no random.
  Two runs must be byte-identical outside `.git`.
- Standard library only, 16,384 bytes or fewer (REQ-6 of `map-on-trial`; accepted at 12,288,
  raised by the owner on 2026-10-07 after three compaction passes).
- No file named `prompt.md` or `case.yaml` under `fixtures/` — the eval tool would read the
  folder as a case.
- The generated tests must pass: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` in the generated tree.
